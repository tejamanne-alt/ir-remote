# Privacy policy (static page; link it from both listings and the About screen)

_Last updated: 2026-11-09._

- The app has no account, no ads and no analytics.
- Your remotes, AC settings and learned codes are stored only on your device.
  Backups are files you create and share yourself.
- The app talks to IR hubs on your local network. The only internet request
  is an optional download of extra code sets from a fixed, hash-verified
  address. No personal data is sent.
- Crash reporting is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, reports
  exclude personal data.
- Publisher: Astradite. Contact: support address to be added before
  publication.
